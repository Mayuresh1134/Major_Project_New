What to write down
Rhythm

The shortest note the sheet music uses. Coarser is tidier.

{{ range_input("sensitivity", "Sensitivity", 0, 1, 0.05, 0.5, "", "Higher finds quieter notes, and more wrong ones.") }}

Works best with

  • One instrument or voice, recorded clearly.
  • Piano, guitar, flute, voice and other instruments with a clear start to each note.

Dense band or orchestra recordings come out as many notes at once; turn on Melody only for those. Bowed strings and organ are recognised poorly.

Notes are found with Spotify's open-source Basic Pitch model.
