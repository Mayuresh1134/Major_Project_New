About the model

A small recurrent network (an LSTM) that looks at the last 25 notes and predicts the next note's pitch, timing and length. It was trained on piano music following TensorFlow's music generation tutorial, so it writes in a loose piano style.

The three options above are rules applied on top of the model. Turn them off to hear it unrestricted.
