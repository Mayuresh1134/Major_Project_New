Quality

Standard takes about 20 seconds for 15 seconds of audio on a typical laptop; thorough takes twice as long.

How it works

Both clips become spectrograms. A layer of fixed random filters describes each clip's texture as correlations between its filter responses. The content clip is then nudged, step by step, until its texture matches the style clip's, and turned back into sound.

Results sound like a blend of tone colours, not like a different performer: melody and rhythm stay those of the content clip. Expect some metallic artefacts.
