Good to know

  • A photo or PDF of printed music can't be read. Export MusicXML from your notation program instead.
  • MIDI files don't store key or note spelling, so the engraved score may spell some notes differently from the original.
  • Sound comes from MuseAssist's built-in synthesizer.{% if fluidsynth %} FluidSynth and your SoundFont are used for fixed instruments.{% else %} For General MIDI sound, install FluidSynth and put a .sf2 file in the soundfonts folder.{% endif %}
